Television Energy Consumption

Televisions are common household appliances in Australia. Their electricity consumption can vary depending on the screen size, display technology, brightness settings and hours of use.

Modern televisions are generally designed to use less electricity than older models. However, using a television for many hours each day can still contribute to household energy consumption.

Factors That Affect Energy Usage

  • Television screen size
  • Display technology
  • Brightness settings
  • Hours of use per day
  • Standby and power-saving settings
